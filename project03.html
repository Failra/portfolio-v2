Project 3: Biomimicry — Flapping-Wing Bird

For the biomimicry project I used LEGO SPIKE Prime to build a model bird. The goal was to take a structure and function from nature and reproduce the mechanism, so I focused on how a bird flaps its wings.

The build runs on a single small motor. Rather than driving each wing separately, the motor's rotation is converted into the up-and-down flapping of both wings at once, so one input drives the whole motion. The video above shows the bird running.
