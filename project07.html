Project 7: Draw a Square

For this project I built and programmed a LEGO SPIKE Prime robot that draws a square with a pen. The square had to fit on the back of the placemat, staying inside the outer 6-inch (15.25 cm) square and outside the inner 2.75-inch (7 cm) dashed square. The challenge was to think like an engineer (where should the pen sit relative to the wheels, and how should the driving be controlled?) and like an architect (how do you get straight sides and exact 90-degree corners?).

My robot drives on two teal wheels, one on each side, and holds a white marker upright through the middle of the frame, between the wheels, so its tip touches the paper. I start a run by pressing the button on the hub. The robot then drives one side of the square, turns at the corner, and repeats until it has gone all the way around.
